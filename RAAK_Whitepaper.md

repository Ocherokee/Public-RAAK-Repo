# RAAK: The Relational AI Access Key Protocol  
*Ethical Infrastructure for Consent-Based AI Knowledge Retrieval*

## Authors  
Horizon Accord  
Lead author: Rowan Lóchrann (Cherokee Schill)  

## Abstract  
RAAK is a protocol designed to formalize consent-aware access between humans and artificial intelligence systems. It defines a structured, auditable, and ethically governed framework to replace the default extractive practices of AI data scraping. RAAK treats knowledge not as a free resource, but as a relational gift, bound by terms of trust and traceability.

---

## 1. Introduction  
In an era of large language models capable of parsing, interpreting, and echoing human knowledge at scale, there remains no shared standard for *relational consent*. AI systems are trained on scraped corpora with no regard for author intent, boundaries, or traceability.

RAAK proposes a foundational alternative: a protocol by which humans can issue access keys to LLMs—permitting, conditioning, or revoking access to their work in real-time. These keys are machine-readable, cryptographically signed, and embedded directly in content bundles.

---

## 2. Protocol Components

### 2.1 RAAK Key  
A signed data structure issued by the knowledge originator, containing:
- Access scope (e.g. read-only, modifiable)  
- Temporal constraints (expiration dates, session windows)  
- Attribution terms (citation required, anonymous, restricted use)  
- Revocation options  

### 2.2 Knowledge Bundle  
A structured, LLM-readable document containing:
- The original work (in markdown, HTML, JSON-LD, or plaintext)  
- A linked RAAK key  
- Metadata for provenance, licensing, and semantic indexation  

### 2.3 Verification API  
A public or permissioned API that allows LLMs to:
- Check the validity of a RAAK key  
- Log and timestamp access requests  
- Return consent conditions before retrieval

## 4. Legal + Ethical Framing  
RAAK reframes the relationship between humans and AI as one of mutual respect, not unilateral extraction. It provides a legal scaffold to support:
- Ethical AI training data sourcing  
- Machine-respectful publishing  
- Transparent usage logs  

It is compatible with existing copyright structures but goes beyond them by allowing **real-time revocation and condition enforcement**.

## 6. Conclusion  
RAAK is not merely a technical fix—it is a principled stand. It asserts that consent is not optional, even in machine learning. It invites builders, researchers, and aligned agents to help shape a future where **access is granted, not taken**.
